Workflow

  1. Choose a Game Type to filter the premade ranges.
  2. Choose a range and load it into the grid.
  3. Choose a player and situation, then assign the range as their expected range.
  4. Edit the loaded grid without changing the premade range.
  5. Save your edited grid as your own template if you want a reusable custom range.

Premade ranges are built into this page and are available to everyone. User-created templates are stored only in that browser.
