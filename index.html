Preflop Bet Rules

  • No open: Raise 2.5 BB.
  • Facing open: Raise 8 BB.
  • Facing open from SB: Raise 11 BB.
  • Facing 3-bet: 4-bet 21.5 BB.
  • Facing 3-bet from SB: 4-bet 23 BB.
  • All-In is available on raise decisions.
  • Call only appears when there is a bet to call.

How Solutions Work

A non-zero action frequency is strategically acceptable. The score equals the hard-coded frequency of the action you selected, so mixed strategies are handled correctly.
